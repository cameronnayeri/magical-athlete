How this works

The tournament is a series of races. Before a race there may be a draft (or a create-your-own-racer round first), then everyone picks which racer runs, then you race, then points are handed out.

Drafting goes in snake order (first to last, then last to first). Click a card to read it, then hit Draft.

Racing: on your turn hit Roll, then move your pawn by clicking it and clicking a tile (or drag it). Read your racer's card and apply the ability yourselves — nothing is enforced, and anyone can move any pawn, so abilities that shove other racers are easy to resolve. Hit End turn when you're done.

Board tiles: ladders, chutes, point tiles and trip tiles apply themselves when you land. A ladder tile reads ↑ and its target, a chute tile ↓. Boost and setback tiles (+3, −2) are yours to apply: with the pawn selected, an Apply button shows in the toolbar. Random ones are re-rolled every race. Undo reverses all of it.

Flip card turns over the top card of the undrafted deck for abilities that ask for one, or just for chaos.

Finishing: drop a pawn on the END tile to record its place. The host hits End race when enough racers are in, and can tweak the points before continuing.

Drawing: hit the pen to scribble on the board. Hide drawings if it gets busy. The host can mute anyone who goes rogue.

Shortcuts: type a number to move your pawn that far (a minus sign moves back, Enter applies it right away) · R roll · N end turn · U undo · D draw mode · Esc deselects and closes things.

Cards and boards: edit card text in js/cards.js and boards in js/boards.js.
